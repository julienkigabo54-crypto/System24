Know what to order before you run out.

The Intelligent Inventory System records every stock movement, learns each product's demand pattern from history, predicts the coming weeks and tells you how much extra stock to buy, and how urgent it is.

Open dashboard Manage products Predict demand

How it works

  1. Register products and record stock-in / stock-out. Every movement is saved to the daily history.
  2. Prepare the data. Duplicates and missing values are cleaned, then features are built from the history.
  3. Train the model. Linear Regression (and a Random Forest for comparison) learn demand patterns and are scored with MAE, MSE, RMSE and R².
  4. Predict and decide. Predicted demand is compared with current stock and the reorder level to classify each product and recommend an order quantity.

Stock status rules

Critical Out of stock, or it will run out before a supplier could deliver (lead time).

Low Stock At or below the product's reorder level.

OK Enough stock for the coming period.

Overstock Far more than the coming period needs, so cash is tied up.

Thresholds are defined in config.py.
