Your sales records

The model learns from these records, so they should be tidy. Cleaning removes repeated records and fills in empty fields. Days with no record are treated as days with no sales.

Train the prediction model

Training lets the system study your past sales and learn their patterns. Train it again whenever many new sales have been recorded.

How accurate is the model?

To check accuracy, the most recent 20 percent of days were kept hidden while the model learned. The model then predicted those days, and its predictions were compared with what really happened. The best score in each row is shown in green.

Predicted sales compared with real sales

Each point is one of the hidden days, with all products added together. The closer the lines are, the better the model.

What the model pays attention to

The longer the bar, the more that piece of information influences the prediction.

Training history

Every time the model is trained, its scores are saved here. Lower error values and a higher R-squared score are better.
