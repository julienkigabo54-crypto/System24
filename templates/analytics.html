What do these columns mean?
Total units sold
How many units of the product were sold in the 90 days.
Average sold per day
The total divided by the number of days.
Day-to-day variation (standard deviation)
How much the daily sales usually move up or down from the average. A small number means sales are steady.
Highest in one day
The most units sold on a single day.
Unevenness of sales
The day-to-day variation divided by the average. Near 0 means very steady; above 1 means sales jump around a lot.
Direction
Whether sales have been going up, going down, or staying steady. A change of more than 10 percent over the period counts as going up or down.
